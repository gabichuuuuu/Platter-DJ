# Code of conduct

Platter is a technical project. Its spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews) exist
to build the software, and everyone taking part in them is expected to make that easy for the
others.

## Expected

- Discuss the work: the code, the design, the evidence. Disagree with reasons.
- Assume good faith and answer the question that was asked.
- Accept review. Give review that the author can act on.
- Respect the time of the people who maintain the project without being paid for it.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or attacks on a person instead of an argument.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about who someone is.
- Publishing private information of another person.
- Sexual content or advances.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to the project, and repeated low-effort submissions after being asked
  to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, and block accounts that break
these rules, temporarily or permanently, in proportion to the behaviour. To report a problem,
contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile; reports are kept confidential.
